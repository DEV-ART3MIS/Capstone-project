BudgetIQ – Personal Budget Tracking System 💰

BudgetIQ is a personal finance management application designed to help users track their income, expenses, and overall financial activity in a simple and organized way. The platform enables individuals to manage their budgets effectively and make smarter financial decisions.

The goal of BudgetIQ is to provide users with a clear overview of their spending patterns and help them maintain financial discipline through easy tracking and visualization.

🚀 Features

Income Tracking – Record and monitor all income sources.

Expense Management – Log daily expenses and categorize them.

Budget Planning – Set budgets for different spending categories.

Financial Insights – Analyze spending habits through reports and summaries.

User-Friendly Interface – Simple and intuitive design for easy financial tracking.

🎯 Objective

The objective of BudgetIQ is to simplify personal finance management by providing users with a centralized platform to track their financial activities and improve budgeting habits.
